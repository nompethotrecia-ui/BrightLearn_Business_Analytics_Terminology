# 📘 Business Analytics Terminology Glossary

This project is a research-based glossary developed as part of the **Business Analytics — Foundations** course. It focuses on defining and applying key concepts, metrics, and KPIs that form the foundation of modern data-driven decision-making in business.


## 🎯 Project Overview

The purpose of this assignment is to build a strong understanding of the **language of Business Analytics** by researching and defining 38 essential terms across four key areas:

- Foundational Concepts of Analytics
- Data, Measurement & Reporting
- Customer & Sales KPIs
- Marketing & Campaign Analytics

Each term includes:
- A clear definition (in my own words)
- A real-world business example
- A credible source for reference


## 🧠 Learning Objectives

Through this project, I aimed to:

- Develop a solid understanding of core business analytics terminology
- Differentiate between commonly confused concepts (e.g., KPI vs Metric, CAC vs CLV)
- Understand how businesses use data to drive decisions
- Apply theoretical concepts to real-world business scenarios


## 📂 Project Structure

The glossary is organized into the following sections:

### 1. Foundational Concepts of Analytics
Covers key ideas such as:
- Business Analytics
- Data Analytics
- Business Intelligence (BI)
- Descriptive, Predictive, Diagnostic, and Prescriptive Analytics
- Big Data
- Data Literacy

### 2. Data, Measurement & Reporting
Focuses on how data is structured, stored, and interpreted:
- Structured vs Unstructured Data
- Data Warehouse & Data Mining
- Dashboards & Data Visualization
- Metrics, KPIs, Benchmarks, and Segmentation

### 3. Customer & Sales KPIs
Explores performance metrics used to measure business growth:
- Conversion Rate
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Churn Rate & Retention Rate
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Sales Funnel

### 4. Marketing & Campaign Analytics
Examines how marketing performance is measured:
- Marketing Campaigns
- ROI & ROAS
- Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- Bounce Rate
- A/B Testing
- Lead Generation
- Marketing Attribution


## 🔍 Key Insights

- Not all metrics are KPIs — KPIs are directly tied to business goals.
- The **CLV:CAC ratio** is a critical indicator of business sustainability.
- Data-driven decision-making is essential for modern organizations.
- Marketing analytics relies heavily on connecting multiple metrics to evaluate performance.
